Our Story

Tradition in every single bite

Alice Poulain Café brings the classic French bakery atmosphere directly to you. Every cake, croissant, and quiche is crafted with classical techniques and inspired by timeless Parisian family recipes.


Whether you're stopping by for a quick morning espresso or enjoying a slow lunch, we invite you to experience the authentic taste of Paris.
